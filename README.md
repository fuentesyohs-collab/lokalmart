## Project Overview

LOKALMART is a web-based E-Commerce Online Ordering and Inventory Management System designed for streetwear products. It provides customers with an online platform where they can browse products, explore available collections, and place orders conveniently.

The system also provides administrative features for managing products, customer orders, and inventory.

## Project Objectives

- Develop a user-friendly online shopping platform.
- Provide convenient product browsing and ordering.
- Implement customer account management.
- Provide product and inventory management features.
- Organize online orders and store operations.

## Main System Features

### Customer Features
- Customer Registration and Login
- Product Catalog
- Product Search and Categories
- Product Details
- Shopping Cart
- Checkout
- Order History
- Order Tracking

### Administrator Features
- Admin Dashboard
- Product Management
- Category Management
- Inventory Management
- Order Management
- Customer Management
- Sales Monitoring
